# ConceptNet Regional Intelligence Extension

> ConceptNet — A multidimensional network of regional knowledge, identity, history, and mythological intelligence.

This repository is inspired by and extended from [ConceptNet 5 by Commonsense AI](https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet5).

The project expands the original commonsense knowledge graph into a richer intelligence framework capable of representing regional cultures, civilizations, traditions, historical narratives, folklore, mythology, languages, and collective human knowledge.

---

## Vision

The goal of this project is to build a structured intelligence network that connects:

- Regional knowledge systems
- Cultural identity and traditions
- Historical events and timelines
- Mythological entities and narratives
- Languages and literature
- Geography and civilization patterns
- Folk stories and oral histories
- Semantic and commonsense relationships

By integrating these dimensions, ConceptNet evolves beyond generic commonsense reasoning into a civilization-scale cultural intelligence graph.

---

## Example

A region such as Andhra Pradesh can be represented through interconnected dimensions:

- Historical dynasties and empires
- Mythological narratives surrounding Tirumala Temple
- Telugu literature and linguistic evolution
- Festivals, rituals, cuisine, and performing arts
- Geographic influence of the Krishna and Godavari rivers
- Connections between epics, folklore, and regional identity

## Objectives

- Extend commonsense knowledge graphs with cultural intelligence
- Preserve regional and indigenous knowledge systems
- Build multilingual semantic relationships
- Represent mythology alongside historical context
- Enable AI systems to reason across culture, geography, and history
- Create interoperable cultural knowledge graphs for research and education
